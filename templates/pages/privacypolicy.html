Legal

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information LKProfessionals collects, why we collect it, and how we protect inquiries, project communication, and client-related records.

Information We Collect

We may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, company details, service interests, project requirements, budget ranges, and any information you choose to submit through contact or quotation forms.

How We Use Information

Inquiry data is used to respond to requests, qualify projects, prepare proposals, improve service delivery, and maintain support records. We do not sell inquiry information to unrelated third parties.

Storage and Protection

We use reasonable technical and operational safeguards to protect submitted information. Access is limited to authorized personnel and approved service workflows relevant to delivery or support.

Third-Party Services

Hosting providers, analytics tools, email systems, and communication platforms may process limited data where needed to operate the website and client workflow. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request corrections, updates, or deletion of inquiry data where legally and operationally appropriate. For privacy questions, email info@lkprofessionals.com.
