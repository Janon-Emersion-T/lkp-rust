Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for teams evaluating a new website, custom software platform, SEO engagement, support partner, or business automation rollout.

How does a typical project start?

Most engagements begin with a short discovery conversation, requirement review, and a practical scope recommendation covering deliverables, timeline, and budget direction.

Do you only build websites?

No. We also build internal business systems, portals, custom software, mobile experiences, automation workflows, and growth-supporting digital infrastructure.

Can you improve an existing system instead of rebuilding everything?

Yes. We often audit current websites or systems first, then recommend targeted upgrades, staged modernization, or selective rewrites where that is the better business decision.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Depending on the engagement, we can provide maintenance, hosting coordination, monitoring, updates, and support workflows after the initial delivery phase.
